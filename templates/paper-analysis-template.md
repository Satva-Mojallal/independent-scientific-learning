1. Research question

What did the researchers want to find out?

2. Background and rationale

What was already known, and what gap did the study address?

3. Hypothesis and predictions

What hypothesis did the researchers investigate?

Separate the authors' stated hypothesis from predictions inferred from the experimental logic.

4. Experimental design

What was compared, manipulated, measured, or observed?

Why was this design appropriate for the research question?

5. Methods and measurements

For each major method:

- Why was it used?
- What does it directly measure?
- What are its limitations?
- What conclusions can it support?

6. Controls

What controls were included?

What alternative explanation does each control address?

Were any important controls missing or insufficient?

7. Main results

Summarize the major findings accurately.

Avoid claiming more than the data show.

8. Interpretation

What do the authors conclude?

Do the experimental results support that conclusion?

Is there a difference between what the results directly demonstrate and what the authors propose?

9. Limitations and alternative explanations

What else could explain the observations?

What remains unresolved?

10. Follow-up experiment

Propose an experiment that could distinguish between plausible explanations.

Explain:

- The research question.
- The method.
- The controls.
- The expected outcomes.
- How each possible outcome would be interpreted.

11. Reasoning backwards

Choose one important result.

Ask:

- What experiment generated this result?
- What question was that experiment designed to answer?
- Why might the researchers have selected this method?
- What controls were needed?
- What alternative explanations remain?

12. Personal learning

What did this paper teach me about biology, experimental design, or scientific reasoning?

Which methods or concepts did I understand better after analysing it?

13. Sources and attribution

List the original paper and any additional sources used.

Clearly distinguish information reported by the authors from my own interpretation and critique.
